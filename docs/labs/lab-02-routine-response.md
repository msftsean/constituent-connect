# Lab 02: Grounded routine response

## Objective

Trace a routine inquiry from intake through a cited draft.

## Path

1. Select **Routine license replacement**.
2. Process the inquiry.
3. Confirm the proposed `professional-licensing` route.
4. Open the citation and review the approved public excerpt.
5. Confirm the response is pending human approval.

## Check

The draft has a citation and avoids eligibility, legal, payment, or outcome
promises. Do not approve a draft merely because its wording sounds plausible.
